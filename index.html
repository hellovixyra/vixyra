Powerful Features

Everything you need to create stunning designs

✏️

Rich Text Editing

Add and edit text with 15+ Google Fonts, formatting options, colors, and backgrounds. Full control over typography.

🖼️

Image & Shapes

Upload images, add shapes (rectangles, circles, lines, arrows), and apply filters like blur, brightness, and contrast.

📋

Template Library

Browse pre-made templates for business cards, social media posts, presentations, and more. Save your own templates too!

📚

Layers Panel

Manage layers with show/hide controls, reorder objects, and organize your design elements with ease.

💾

Auto-Save & History

Automatic version history with 30-second auto-save. Restore any previous version with one click.

📤

Multiple Export Formats

Export your designs as PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF with custom dimensions, quality settings, and transparent backgrounds.

📄

Multiple Pages

Create multi-page designs with easy navigation. Perfect for presentations, social media series, and more.

🎨

Advanced Tools

Crop images, flip objects, duplicate elements, apply animations, and use zoom controls from 25% to 400%.

☁️

Cloud Storage

Save projects to cloud storage, generate shareable links, and access your designs from anywhere.
